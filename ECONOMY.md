# ECONOMY & TUNING GUIDE

All values live in data files: `src/data/economy.ts` (rewards), `src/data/vehicles.ts` (truck prices and stats), `src/data/cosmetics.ts` (paints and extras), `src/data/upgrades.ts` (upgrade costs and effects), `src/data/tuning.ts` (handling and stack physics), `src/data/levels.ts` (targets), `src/data/contracts.ts`, `src/data/achievements.ts`.

## One currency
Coins are earned only by playing. There is no premium currency, no real-money checkout, no loot boxes, no energy, and no timers. Retries are always free.

## Earning (per run)
| Source | Value |
|---|---|
| Road coin | 1 each |
| Delivered cargo | item value × condition (0–100%; dents from jolts, squished fragile items) |
| Delivery complete (campaign) | 12 + 2 × level number |
| First time each medal is earned | +20 |
| Near miss / close shave under a beam | +3 |
| Wobble save (recover from critical stability without losing cargo) | +5 |
| Clean landing | +3 |
| Smart shed (shed at critical stability or to clear a beam) | +4 |
| Long Haul / Daily distance | +2 per 100 m |
| Long Haul / Daily depot banking | cargo value × multiplier (×1, ×1.25, ×1.5, ×1.8, ×2.1, ×2.5 cap) |
| Daily Contract completion | +150, once per UTC day |
| Contracts | 60–250 |
| Achievements | 30–400 |

A crash keeps road coins and bonuses. In the campaign, cargo still on board is lost. In Long Haul and the Daily Contract only the **unbanked** cargo (shown in the HUD as "AT RISK") is forfeited; banked coins are safe.

## Spending
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Trucks | Van 1,800 · Trike 2,400 · Flatbed 3,800 · 4×4 5,200 (Pickup free) |
| Paints (13 + free Tomato) | 350–1,300, Golden Gnome 3,000 |
| Extras (7, one per slot) | 300–1,500 |
| Suspension / Tire Grip upgrades | 300, 600, 1,000, 1,600, 2,500 |
| Cargo Supports upgrade | 350, 700, 1,100, 1,700, 2,700 |
| Spare Tire (continue once per run, max 3) | 140 |

**First meaningful purchase:** the cheapest upgrade (300) or paint (350) is affordable after 2 ordinary early runs (~140 + ~170). A first truck (Van, 1,800) takes roughly 8–10 runs. Buying everything costs about 48,000 coins, which is around 200–240 typical runs. That is steady long-term progression without walls.

## Upgrades are modest by design
At tier 5, the maximum benefits are: roll resistance +30%, jolt absorption +25%, steering response +25%, puddle resistance up to 90%, stack stiffness +30%, slide tolerance +20%. Upgrades widen the margin for error but cannot stop a sloppy swerve with a tall stack. Upgrades are disabled in the Daily Contract. Cosmetics are visual only, and `applyUpgrades(stats, tiers)` takes no cosmetic input (enforced by `tests/unit/progression.test.ts`).

## Truck identities
| Truck | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Pip Pickup (starter) | Balanced | No special tricks. Viable for every level (validated) |
| Bramble Box Van | Cargo walls lock the bottom item; high roll stiffness | Slower steering |
| Zippi Trike | Fastest steering, narrowest body | Tips easily |
| Longbed Hauler | Two cargo columns (14 items), low stacks | Wide, slowest steering |
| Bruiser 4×4 | Soaks up bumps, landings and puddles | High cargo floor (taller stacks) |

## Stack physics knobs (`src/data/tuning.ts`)
- `stackK` (55): base interface stiffness. Higher means less wobble.
- `stackDampRatio` (0.22): how quickly wobble settles.
- `stackLever` (0.3/m): how much more higher items swing.
- `stackLoadStiffen` (0.55): how much weight above stiffens lower items.
- `slipFraction` (0.42): how far an item may slide (fraction of the supporting half-width) before it falls.
- `feelSteer` (0.12), `feelCorner` (0.42), `feelLag` (0.1 s): how much steering and corners reach the cargo.
- `tipGain` (1.3), `rolloverLtr` (1.0), `wheelLiftLtr` (0.72), `rolloverTime` (0.45 s): rollover behaviour.

Reference behaviour (`tests/tools/diag.tool.ts`, pickup at 16 m/s): a full-steer double lane change peaks at 30% of the slip limit with 1 item, 63% with 3, 79% with 5 and 97% with 7 (nearly falls). A 55 m-radius corner peaks at 20%, 45%, 56% and 69% respectively.
